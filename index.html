Round 9 — the real one

RC

Radio Change

The actual interface. One page, two states: off air it is an under-inked photocopy; pressing play floods it with ink. Real Web Audio chain — play/stop, tall volume fader, 10-band EQ with presets, audio-reactive wordmark and scope. Live on the real stream, NOW PLAYING polled straight off the Shoutcast server.

Round 8 — a real player

15

Webamp

Winamp 2 reimplemented for the browser, running inside the zine environment. Real audio, real 10-band EQ, real transport. Default skin is a placeholder — the custom skin is the design job.

Round 7 — maximalist

14

Maximal

54 pieces instead of 20. Ink fields, newsprint scraps, extra halftone cut-outs, repeated-name tickers, stickers, hand-drawn arrows and scribbles, marginalia. Only the letters react to the cursor.

Round 6 — too stark

13

Cut-up

Ransom-note letters on scraps and knocked out of ink blocks, 10–20° rotations, heavy overlap, tape, staples, fold crease, crop marks, copier grime. Scraps shy away from the cursor.
