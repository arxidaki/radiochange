The icon: the R and the C as a sticker of their own

ICON

The icon

The R and the C lifted out of the sprayed wordmark and cut as a sticker of their own, on the page's foil, with the page's hard shadow, standing on the page's own ground. As an app icon, as a phone and a launcher cut it, and as the favicon at 16, 32 and 48, each drawn for its size.

Sketch · for the home screen and the tab

The foil: what makes the sticker's shine look real

FOIL

The foil

The sticker in the header as holographic foil that looks like the real thing, after a photograph of real stickers: brushed, with tiny polygons pressed into it. Strong colour, a cool pale silver where the light glares, the fine grain of the paper, and polygons that each stand a little apart from their neighbours. The one kept from a sheet of nine. The pointer tilts it. The sticker stays put, and only its shine moves.

Sketch · brushed, with tiny polygons

The first touch: asking for the tap that starts the sound

CUE

The first touch

A browser holds the sound back until the listener touches the page, and the small pill that asks for that touch is easy to miss. A sheet of sketches: the page as it stands and four other ways to ask, each on a phone and on a desk. A bar at the foot of the page, a band across the song, the mute drop grown big, and the page turned down until the sound is on. At the top, the one picked: the last three together, the page turned down behind one big amber button that says tap to start.

Cover art: covers that bring an outline of their own

EDGE 2

The cover's edge, found by the page

The page measures each cover's outline for itself. A cover with an outline all the way round is magnified by just enough to lose it, each side cut where its own frame ends; any other is left as it is. Eighteen covers, each as it is now and as the page would show it, with what the page made of it. One slider sets the most that may come off a side.

Found by the page · magnified only when needed · a few milliseconds a cover In the player, with the limit at 4%
EDGE 1

The cover's edge

A square outline on a cover gets cut short by the slot's round corners. Two ways to lose it, each beside the slot as it is now: a border on the inside of the slot, or the picture a little bigger than the slot. Seven covers, picked for what they have at the edge. One slider sets how much comes off each side, for both at once; the cover can be seen at its size on a computer or on a phone.

Border inside · picture magnified · the same for every cover

Round 12: warmer colours

W1

Ember

All fire: gold through orange and vermilion to pink, plum and wine, on a wine-dark ink. No blue and no green anywhere. Sound off, it banks down to maroon and burnt orange.

Warmest · no cool colours
W2

Sundown

Today's palette with the blues and the teal taken out: the warm half stays, and purples do the dark. Sound off, it is purple, rose and wine where it was indigo.

Closest to now · warm with purple
W3

Harvest

Earth: burnt orange, pumpkin and mustard against leaf greens, on a coffee brown. Sound off, it is rust, olive and burgundy.

Earthy · warm with green
All three are one page, the player as it stands with only its colours changed. The names in its header (or the keys 1 to 4) change the colours while it plays; Now is today's palette, to compare against.

Round 11: the three that landed, together

RC

Radio Change

Opens straight on the song that is playing and goes on air by itself; there is no play button, only mute. Liquid is the ground, Billboard sets the artist and title edge to edge, and the field is ten columns lit to each band's level, with one more behind the volume for the level going out. Presets sit in the footer; Custom EQ settles the liquid into ten flat bars, the test card's way: each bar is its own slider, with one line across it. Cover for each song with the last songs played under it, and an upright volume fader the mouse wheel moves.

Auto on air · mute · cover art · just played · presets · custom EQ · vertical volume

Logo: symbol sketches, in 3D

LOGO

Five symbols for change in life

One bold shape each, built as a lit object on a dark glossy floor: a dandelion seed, a door left ajar, an hourglass, a record on the horizon, a delta bent from one bar of metal. They move, and turn with the pointer. Sketches for pointing at; none is finished.

Round 10: a new approach

01

Billboard

The song is the page. Artist and title are set edge to edge by stretching the letterforms, each line is a level meter, and every track gets its own colours.

Kept: the type that stretches to any length
03

Test Card

Off air it is the broadcast colour bars. On air the same ten bars are the spectrum, and dragging one boosts or cuts that band. The centre circle is PLAY, then a scope.

Kept: the EQ you drag, and the presets
04

Liquid

A light show in flat colour: big fluid shapes with hard edges, no blur. Three dusk colours off air; on air the full palette blooms out of the PLAY drop and the bass churns it.

Kept: the animation
02

Signal

PLAY is the transmitter. Every kick and snare sends out a new ring of colour.

Out: the rings are nauseating
