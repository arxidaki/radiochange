The first touch

A browser holds the sound back until the listener has touched the page, so on a first visit the page has to ask. Today it asks in a small pill at the foot of the page, and that is easy to miss. First the one picked, and under it the sketches it came from. Each picture is the page itself. Open one and touch it: the sound starts, and the cue goes the way it would.
