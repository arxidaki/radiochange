The foil

The sticker in the header as holographic foil: brushed, with tiny polygons pressed into it. Warm silver where no colour shows, strong colour where it does, the fine grain of the paper, and polygons that each stand a little apart from their neighbours. Move the pointer anywhere on this page: it tilts the sticker. The sticker itself stays put, and only its shine moves.

Brushed, with tiny polygons

Big, to look at, and under it at the size it has on the page.

On the page the shine would answer only to a pointer on the sticker or near it, as it does now, and drift by itself the rest of the time.
