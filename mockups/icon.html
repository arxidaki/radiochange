The icon

The R and the C of the name, lifted out of the sprayed wordmark and made a sticker of their own: cut to their shape, on the page's foil, with the page's hard shadow, standing on the page's own ground. It is the sticker in the header, made square. The foil is a still of the one on the page: the same spectrum, the same tiny polygons, the grain and one band of light.

The app icon

For a home screen. A phone rounds the corners itself, so the picture is a plain square. Some launchers cut their own shape out of the middle, and for those there is a second picture with more room around the sticker, so nothing of it is lost.

The favicon

For a tab and a bookmark. There is no room for the ground at that size, so the sticker is as wide as the square, and the square's corners are rounded. One file holds it at 16, 32 and 48, each drawn for its size: the smaller it is, the thicker the strokes and the narrower the border, and the splatter is left off.

The other way, not taken

Foil from edge to edge and the two letters painted on it. The letters are bigger, but the cut edge and the hard shadow are gone, and with them what makes it the sticker on the page. It is here to compare.
