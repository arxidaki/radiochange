The cover's edge, found by the page

The page looks at every cover as it arrives and measures the outline it came with, if it has one. A cover with an outline all the way round is magnified by just enough to lose it, each side cut where its own frame ends, and no side by more than the limit set here. A cover with no outline, with one wider than the limit, or with one the page cannot make out on every side, is left as it is.
